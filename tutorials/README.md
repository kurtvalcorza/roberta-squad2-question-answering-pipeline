## What changed from the inference-only tutorial

The previous notebook (`TASK-INFERENCE`) answered three authored questions over one synthetic passage and reported `sample-sanity` exact-match/F1 for single pairs. This notebook keeps that inference contract as Section 5 and adds the adaptation contract around it: a real gold-span corpus chosen because the frozen model fails it, corpus-level metrics in the carried `metrics.py`, two non-neural baselines, a fit check that drops rather than truncates, a bounded fine-tuning, and an artifact that reloads. The per-pair `evaluation_report` helper is unchanged; `pipe.evaluate` is the corpus-level stage (`measured` / `measured-small-sample`).

## Conformance notes

- **Standalone carrier (ST1–ST8):** the primary path performs no clone, repository install, or repository import. Section 2 embeds the repository's 3 modules verbatim in topological dependency order (each tagged `metadata.dimer.embedded_module`; the only rewrites make `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS_DIR` working-directory-relative and remove internal relative package imports), Section 3 carries the 7-file snapshot manifest inline and asserts it against the module's constants before fetching, and `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE` records the repository, revision and module digests in the exports.
- **Parity (PAR1–PAR4):** `tests/test_notebook_parity.py` and `tools/validate_release_assets.py` fail when the carried modules, the inline manifest, or the inline pins differ from the repository, or when the notebook is not byte-identical to the generator's output.
- **Model acquisition (MOD1–MOD9):** `stage_missing_files(WEIGHTS_DIR, allow_download=True)` fetches only the manifest entries a fresh clone lacks (the git-ignored `model.safetensors`), at the pinned revision; `verify_snapshot` re-hashes every entry; `RoBERTaQuestionAnsweringPipeline.from_pretrained(weights_dir=WEIGHTS_DIR)` loads the verified files (`local_files_only=True`, `trust_remote_code=False`); no pickle is opened anywhere; no cell outside the carried modules calls `huggingface_hub`, `transformers`, `safetensors` or `urllib`.
- **Data (DAT1–DAT9, ST6):** the default sample is one pinned object — `aqa_v1.0.zip` (9,018,914 bytes, SHA-256 `f4f3c232…`) from `adversarialqa.github.io` — refused on any mismatch before it is read, read member by member with no `extractall` (only `3_droberta/train.json` and `3_droberta/dev.json`), filtered to answerable records with every gold span found at its offset and passages of at most 1,300 characters, and cut into 1,000 training questions (a seeded draw from the training pool) plus 200 validation / 400 test questions from the dev member **split by article** (`check_split_disjoint` asserts no shared passage). The template adds a second *External access* bullet naming the host, the byte count and the CC BY-SA 3.0 licence.
- **Fit check (VAL6):** the token ceilings need the real tokenizer, so after the model loads `pipe.check_fit` partitions each split into fitting records and the ids `answer` would refuse; the dropped ids are printed and recorded in the evaluation report (the build record dropped 1 of 1,600), and nothing is truncated or windowed.
- **Stages (§4.1):** `fetch_corpus` + `read_corpus` + `build_sample_dataset` / `load_byod_dataset` + `split_dataset` (sample acquisition) → `validate_dataset` + `check_split_disjoint` + refusal probes (validation, with three digests) → `check_fit` → `validate_inputs` + `pipe.answer` with sanity checks (inference contract) → `null_baseline` + `lexical_overlap_baseline` + `pipe.evaluate` on the frozen model (baselines) → `pipe.adapt` (bounded fine-tuning) → `pipe.evaluate` on validation and test with the comparison (evaluation) → `pipe.answer` on unseen questions + `pipe.evaluate` + `evaluation_report` (inference) → `pipe.save_artifact` → `RoBERTaQuestionAnsweringPipeline.from_artifact` with the parity assertion (export and reload).
- **Score semantics (UNC1–UNC4):** every per-answer score is a product of two softmax masses, not a calibrated probability; exact-match and F1 are corpus-level agreement with gold spans under the SQuAD 2.0 normalisation (own implementations); `answered_rate` is reported beside them; no acceptance threshold or no-answer offset ships. Six unseen questions are scored with a `measured-small-sample` verdict that names the missing dispersion.
- **Baselines and evidence (EVAL6/EVAL10/EVAL11):** the always-null and lexical-overlap baselines and the frozen model are scored on the same 400 test questions as the adapted model, and every metric is labelled as one seeded split of one corpus with no dispersion estimate; the notebook says explicitly that the adaptation trades the null decision away on a corpus with no unanswerable questions.
- **Split integrity (SPL1–SPL8):** the split is seeded, passage-disjoint and (for the sample) article-disjoint between validation and test; validation selects the epoch and the test split is used for nothing but the final evaluation; the notebook states that questions from one document must be split by that document.
- **Ceilings (VAL6):** `MAX_QUESTION_CHARS` (500), `MAX_CONTEXT_CHARS` (3,000), `MAX_QUESTION_TOKENS` (64), `MAX_CONTEXT_TOKENS` (384, rejected not windowed), `MAX_ANSWER_TOKENS` (64), `DEFAULT_MAX_ANSWER_TOKENS` (15), the record bounds (8..20,000) and the id pattern are stated in the Prerequisites and printed before the model runs; four dataset refusals and one settings refusal are demonstrated.
- **BYOD (DAT10–DAT19):** `USE_BYOD` defaults to `False` so the sample path never opens an upload dialog; when enabled, user records flow through the same validation, passage-disjoint split, fit check, baselines, fine-tuning, evaluation, inference, export and reload cells. The expected schema and the privacy guidance — an internal document set with its question log is restricted data — are stated in the Prerequisites and in Section 4, and the training split is written to `outputs/roberta_question_answering_train.csv` in the CSV shape.
- The pinned snapshot is a 7-file manifest with no `tokenizer.json`: `AutoTokenizer` builds `RobertaTokenizerFast` from `vocab.json` + `merges.txt`; the notebook's opening **Snapshot note** says so, and Section 3 stages and digest-verifies those seven files before the tokenizer or the model is constructed.
- Licence: the weights are CC-BY-4.0 and the notebook, README, card and `docs/WEIGHTS.md` carry the attribution to deepset the licence requires; the export records it under `model_attribution`. The corpus is CC BY-SA 3.0 and is not redistributed; the export records its identity, digest and licence under `corpus`.
- `tools/validate_release_assets.py` performs source validation only. It does not satisfy the clean-runtime execution requirement; a release review must confirm that a recorded clean run in `../docs/release-verification.md` matches the notebook revision under review before the status is promoted to `Release-grade`.
